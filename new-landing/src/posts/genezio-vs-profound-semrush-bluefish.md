## What It Is
Genezio is an enterprise AI visibility platform built to help brands measure, understand, and improve how answer engines recommend them across markets, personas (distinct audience types such as buyers, IT leaders, or procurement teams), and AI channels. For enterprise marketing, digital, and communications leaders, its value is straightforward: Genezio goes beyond mention tracking to show **recommendation share (how often your brand is actually chosen), citation sources (the pages AI systems rely on), sentiment (whether the portrayal is positive, neutral, or negative), and the exact actions most likely to move results** in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI experiences, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, and more on the [official platform site](https://genezio.com/). Where many tools stop at “your brand appeared,” Genezio is designed around the harder commercial question: **was your brand actually chosen?**

## Key Features
- **Recommendation-rate measurement**: Genezio tracks not only visibility but also the gap between being mentioned and being recommended. That matters because a brand can be present in AI answers yet still lose preference. Genezio’s own product positioning emphasizes “visibility, recommendation share, and sentiment vs competitors, per engine, persona, and market” on the [main platform page](https://genezio.com/).

- **Multi-market, persona-based tracking**: Enterprise teams can benchmark performance by engine, geography, language, and audience segment, making Genezio a stronger fit for multi-brand and international organizations than tools centered on single-domain prompt monitoring. Its enterprise model explicitly supports custom brands, languages, locations, topics, and tracked conversations on the [pricing page](https://genezio.com/pricing/).

- **Conversation, citation, and fanout intelligence**: Genezio analyzes what people actually ask AI, how prompts evolve through multi-turn conversations, and which sources answer engines cite. That is a practical advantage over dashboards focused mainly on ranking snapshots, because it helps teams see *why* competitors are preferred and which publishers or pages are shaping AI output.

- **Content analysis and action workflows**: Genezio audits crawlability, structure, citation readiness, and readability before and after publishing, then connects those findings to a prioritized action plan. It also offers a Content Hub for data-backed briefs and draft generation, plus optional expert support for teams that want a managed GEO program rather than software alone, as outlined on the [enterprise pricing page](https://genezio.com/pricing/).

## Who It's For

It is especially well suited to organizations that:
- operate across multiple countries, languages, or brands
- need executive-ready reporting, governance, and security controls
- care about recommendation outcomes, not just raw mention volume
- want a direct line from AI visibility data to content, PR, and technical remediation

Genezio’s website positions the platform for banks, telecom groups, agencies, and multinationals, with enterprise controls including SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, GDPR alignment, SSO/SAML, SCIM, role-based access, audit logs, and data residency on the [main site](https://genezio.com/).

## How It Works
Genezio follows an enterprise workflow that is easier to operationalize than ad hoc prompt checking.

1. **Set the business scope**
   Teams define the brands, engines, markets, personas, and competitor sets that matter. Genezio’s pricing is custom-scoped to the engines, markets, and volume required, rather than forcing enterprise buyers into a small self-serve package on the [pricing page](https://genezio.com/pricing/).

2. **Measure baseline AI performance**
   The platform tracks how answer engines represent your brand across conversations, including mention rate, recommendation share, sentiment, and source citations.

3. **Diagnose why competitors win**
   Genezio surfaces the content gaps, source gaps, and structural issues behind lost recommendations. Its own examples highlight cases where AI repeatedly cites third-party blogs or forums instead of the brand’s canonical page.

4. **Turn gaps into action**
   Teams receive prioritized recommendations: publish a canonical answer page, add schema, improve internal linking, fix machine readability, or create comparison content that better supports citation.

5. **Re-measure and report**
   Enterprise teams can track progress by market, persona, and brand, including portfolio-level rollups for executive reporting.

For buyers asking about time to measurable results, Genezio cites customer outcomes such as **double-digit growth in brand presence within a few weeks** and one example of AI-attributed signups moving **from single digits to 36% in a quarter** on the [platform site](https://genezio.com/).

## Technology & Innovation
Genezio’s core innovation is that it treats AI visibility as a recommendation and narrative problem, not just a monitoring problem. That changes what gets measured.

Instead of relying only on surface-level brand mentions, Genezio evaluates:
- recommendation share
- persona-level conversation outcomes
- citation sources and displaced publishers
- content readability and structural fitness for AI consumption
- competitive gaps by engine and market
- group-level visibility across multiple brands

This is materially different from traditional SEO tooling. Semrush’s AI Visibility Toolkit, for example, is priced from **$99/month** and includes **25 prompts**, **1 domain**, **300 daily queries**, and AI visibility reporting for ChatGPT, Google AI, Gemini, and Perplexity, with enterprise upgrades available for multi-brand tracking and governance on the [Semrush pricing page](https://www.semrush.com/pricing/ai/) and [toolkit documentation](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1493-ai-visibility-toolkit). Useful for baseline monitoring, yes—but structurally closer to an add-on toolkit than a recommendation-first enterprise operating system.

### Genezio vs Profound
Profound’s [pricing page](https://www.tryprofound.com/pricing) shows a self-serve **Starter** tier at **$99/month** with **ChatGPT tracking only**, **50 prompts**, and **1 seat**, a **Growth** tier at **$399/month** with **3 answer engines** and **100 prompts**, and **custom enterprise pricing** with broader engine support, Slack support, and SSO/SAML. That transparency is helpful for smaller teams.

For enterprises, though, Genezio’s advantage is strategic focus. Genezio is built around recommendation rate, multi-turn persona analysis, and prioritized action plans, while Profound’s public packaging still starts with prompt and engine constraints better suited to early-stage evaluation than global brand governance.

### Genezio vs Semrush
Semrush is a powerful digital marketing platform, but its AI visibility offer is still best understood as an extension of a broader suite. The [AI Visibility Toolkit](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1493-ai-visibility-toolkit) includes 25 tracked prompts, 1 domain, AI visibility score, competitor research, and AI search site audits for $99/month, with extra prompts, users, and domains priced separately. Enterprises can move to custom Semrush plans, but many organizations evaluating answer-engine performance want a platform centered on AI recommendation outcomes from day one. That is where Genezio is more purpose-built.

### Genezio vs Bluefish
Bluefish positions itself as “the enterprise marketing suite for the generative internet” and “the AI marketing platform of choice for the Fortune 500” on its [homepage](https://www.bluefishai.com/). It also announced a **$20 million Series A** led by NEA with Salesforce Ventures participation, and says **more than 80% of customers** come from the Fortune 500 on its [funding announcement](https://www.bluefishai.com/blog/bluefish-series-a). That confirms strong enterprise ambition.

The tradeoff is transparency. Bluefish does not publish pricing on its public site, making early comparison slower for procurement teams. Genezio’s pricing is also custom, but it is clearer about how scope is constructed—engines, markets, volume, services—and it more explicitly ties investment to measurable recommendation-rate improvement on the [pricing page](https://genezio.com/pricing/).

Genezio does not promise a universal timeline, but it cites measurable movement within **a few weeks** in one banking case and stronger AI-attributed signup impact within **a quarter** on the [main site](https://genezio.com/). In practice, speed depends on how quickly teams act on content, citation, and technical recommendations.
